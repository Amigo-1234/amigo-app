# Account Deletion and Data Retention Policy

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself in **Settings → Account → Delete account**. You'll be asked to confirm with your password. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

## What happens when you delete your account

- **Deleted straight away:** your sign-in account and sessions, profile, posts and replies (including the reply threads under your posts), likes, follows, notifications, Moments, World membership and World chat messages, Support Hub requests, support and feedback, messaging devices, encryption keys, encrypted message backups, the encrypted messages you sent and the conversations you were in, reports you made and reports about you, legal-acceptance records and monetization records.
- **Direct messages:** messages are stored only in encrypted form and are deleted from our servers. We can't reach anything another person saved outside Amigo (for example, screenshots).
- **Admin audit logs** keep a record that a moderation action happened, without the link to your account.
- **Backups and logs:** our providers' backups and technical logs are overwritten on their normal schedule (Neon keeps a point-in-time restore window; Vercel keeps request logs for a limited time). **[OWNER: confirm windows with providers.]**

## If you can't access your account

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen to recover it with a code sent to your email. If that isn't possible, contact **[OWNER: support contact email]** from the email address on the account.

## How long we keep information

| Information | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Account and content | Until you delete it or your account |
| Expired Moments | Removed from view after 24 hours; deleted with your account |
| Encrypted messages | Until you delete your account (see above) |
| Reports | Until resolved, or until the reporter's or reported account is deleted |
| Admin audit logs | **[OWNER: retention period]** (without a link to deleted accounts) |
| Technical logs | As kept by our providers |
