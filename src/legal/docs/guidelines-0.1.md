# Community Guidelines

Amigo is for spending time with the people you actually like. These guidelines apply to everything on Amigo: posts, replies, Moments, profiles, Worlds, Support Hub and messages.

## Be respectful

- No harassment, bullying, threats or targeted abuse.
- No hate speech or content attacking people for who they are (for example race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation).
- Don't share someone's private information (address, phone number, private photos) without their consent.

## Keep people safe

- No content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children. We report such content to the relevant authorities where required.
- No content promoting or glorifying violence, self-harm, terrorism or dangerous activities. If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services.
- No sexually explicit content.

## Be authentic

- Don't impersonate people, brands or organisations. Parody must be clearly labelled.
- Don't use fake accounts, automation or coordinated activity to inflate likes, follows, Support Hub support or World points. Support should come from real people who actually looked at a project.
- Official Amigo badges are given by Amigo only.

## No spam or scams

- No spam, repetitive posting, misleading links, phishing, malware or get-rich-quick schemes.
- Support Hub requests must link to legitimate projects you're entitled to promote.

## Respect others' rights

- Only post content you created or have permission to share. See the [Copyright Policy](/legal/copyright).

## Follow the law

- Don't use Amigo for anything illegal.

## Reporting and enforcement

- Use **Report** on posts, Moments, Support Hub requests and conversations, or block people you don't want to hear from.
- Moderators review reports and may remove content, warn, restrict, suspend or close accounts depending on how serious and repeated the issue is.
- For encrypted messages, moderators can see only the messages a reporter chooses to share.
