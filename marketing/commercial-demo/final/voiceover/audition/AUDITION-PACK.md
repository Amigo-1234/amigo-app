# Amigo World: voiceover audition pack (male, Nigerian English)

**For casting only. Nothing has been sent, booked or paid.**

## Brief

A 1:45 launch commercial for **Amigo World**, a social app made in Lagos. The visuals are friends, a small business, a musician, and a city sharing one moment. The narration is 17 short lines over music.

**Voice:** male; warm and naturally deep; mature and confident. A natural Nigerian English accent, subtle and easy to understand internationally. Conversational, with real emotion: a person telling a story that matters to him, not reading an ad. Keep natural breaths and pauses. No presenter voice, no "advert" tone.

**Human recording only:** no AI voices, text-to-speech, synthetic narration or voice cloning, in the audition or the final.

## Audition request

1. Read the **full script** below: 17 lines, about 45 seconds of speech.
2. Record **two passes**: a natural read, then a warmer, more intimate read.
3. **Format:** WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono. Dry, with no music, EQ, compression or noise reduction. Include 3 s of room tone at the start.
4. **Optional:** read to picture with the guide video (`AmigoWorld_VO-guide_picture-plus-music_720p.mp4`, which has music and effects only). Start each line at its timecode.
5. **Confirm in writing** that the audition is your own unedited human voice, and that no AI, text-to-speech or voice-cloning tool was used.

**Read notes**
- **Lines 1–5:** quiet observation, almost to yourself.
- **Line 6 ("Imagine…"):** the turn. Lift, with hope.
- **Lines 7–11:** warm and conversational, a smile in the voice.
- **Line 9 ("Talk freely…"):** the longest. Unhurried, reassuring, about 4 seconds.
- **Lines 12–14:** build. Pause after "comes…", then "everyone shows up." lands on the beat. "Live. Together. In the same World." is three deliberate beats.
- **Line 15:** simple and proud.
- **Lines 16–17:** a close friend's sign-off; "today" is an invitation, not a push.
