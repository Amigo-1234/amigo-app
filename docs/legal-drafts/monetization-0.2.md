# Monetization Programme Terms

> **Creator monetization is not available yet.** No one can earn money on Amigo today, and nothing on Amigo should be read as a promise of earnings.

## What's planned

Amigo may in future offer a programme that lets eligible creators earn from their work. If it launches:

- the eligibility rules, any thresholds, how earnings would be calculated and how payments would be made will be published here **before** the programme opens;
- creators will apply, and every application will be reviewed — reaching a number (followers, help received, posts) will not by itself make anyone eligible;
- eligibility may consider account standing, authentic engagement from real, verified accounts, help received through Help Hub, regular original activity and compliance with the [Community Guidelines](/legal/guidelines);
- manipulation — fake accounts, bought or automated engagement, Help Hub or Kudos farming — will make an account ineligible and may lead to suspension.

## Help Hub

Help Hub has no monetary fees; asking for help costs Kudos, as described in section 7 of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). Kudos are recognition points only: they have no cash value, aren't money or earnings, and can't be bought, sold, transferred, withdrawn or paid out. Help counts and Kudos are a community signal; they may be one of the factors considered for future eligibility, but they don't guarantee payment, promotion or any other reward.

## Status and review

Amigo may mark creators as eligible, not eligible or suspended, and will keep a record of those decisions. You can ask for a decision to be reviewed by contacting **olamidesamson841@gmail.com**.
